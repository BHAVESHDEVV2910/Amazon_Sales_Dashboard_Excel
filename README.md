# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize sales performance using data-driven insights.

This project demonstrates practical skills in **Microsoft Excel, data analysis, data visualization, dashboard creation, and business intelligence**. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual reports that help understand sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Identify important sales trends and patterns.
* Analyze product and category performance.
* Understand sales distribution across different segments.
* Create interactive and easy-to-understand visualizations.
* Convert raw data into meaningful business insights.
* Build a professional Excel dashboard suitable for business reporting.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project focuses on extracting useful insights from sales data, including:

### 1. Sales Analysis

Analysis of sales performance to understand overall business growth and revenue patterns.

### 2. Product Analysis

Identification of products and categories contributing to sales performance.

### 3. Trend Analysis

Analysis of sales patterns over time to identify increases, decreases, and significant trends.

### 4. Category Performance

Comparison of different product categories to understand their contribution to overall sales.

### 5. Business Insights

The dashboard helps convert raw sales records into actionable information that can support business decision-making.
